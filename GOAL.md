### Team Identity — Goal

#### Outcome

Become the artefact a new team produces in its first week.

#### Why this app can win it

Team formation is a universal moment — every new team, every reorg, every
intake — and it has no good free tool. A charter is also inherently shareable:
it exists to be shown to other people, which makes each use its own distribution.

#### Signals

1. Charters created, and charters revisited later for revision.
2. Members per charter — whole-team use rather than one person filling a form.
3. Charters shared or exported outside the app.
4. GitHub stars and use in onboarding material.

#### Boundaries

- Not a wiki or a document editor.
- Not an org chart or a directory.
- A charter must stay producible in one sitting.

*Repo: `agile-toolkit/team-identity`*

---

## Latitude

Every goal above names an outcome, not a mechanism. Any roadmap that moves a
signal without crossing a boundary is fair game — including approaches nobody
in this category has tried. Invention is expected, not merely permitted.
